FOCUS TIMER - App Overview and Features
Version 7.3

==================================================================
ABOUT THE APP
==================================================================
Focus Timer is a calm, easy-to-use Android app for studying and
focused work. It combines timers, Pomodoro, a stopwatch, alarms,
projects with study hours, background sounds and detailed history
in one place, with a soft "clay" design.

- Light and dark theme, with 4 accent colors (purple, orange,
  green, blue)
- Available in 10 languages: English, Persian, Turkish, Arabic,
  German, Spanish, Chinese, Korean, Portuguese and French
  (right-to-left layout for Persian and Arabic)
- A short welcome screen for new users explains the basics
- Five tabs: Timers, Projects, Stopwatch, History, Settings


==================================================================
1. TIMERS
==================================================================
Three kinds of timers:
- Timer: one countdown of any length
- Pomodoro: focus / short break / long break, with rounds
  (lengths and rounds can be customized)
- Sequence: your own list of steps (work and rest), repeated for
  a number of rounds

Creating a timer (the New timer window):
- One-tap lengths: 5, 10, 15, 25, 30, 45 min, 1 h, 1h 30m
- Hour / minute / second wheels for any other length
- New timers start at 25 minutes, ready to start
- Optional name and project

On the Timers screen:
- Quick-start buttons for timers you used recently
- Filter timers by project
- Each timer card shows the time, a progress ring, its status and
  when it ends
- Start / Pause button and a +1 min button
- Menu: Reset, Edit, Pin to top, Delete
- Stopping a timer early still saves the time you studied
- When the list is empty: "Start 25-min focus" and "New timer"
  buttons
- An alarm-clock button opens Alarms, and a chip shows the next
  alarm ("07:30 - in 9h 12m")

When a timer ends:
- Full-screen "Time's up" screen, also over the lock screen
- +1 min, Restart and Stop buttons
- Add a note about what you studied
- Your chosen alarm sound plays until stopped


==================================================================
2. ALARMS
==================================================================
Alarms page:
- "Next alarm" card with an analog clock face, the time and how
  long until it rings ("Tomorrow - in 9h 12m")
- Quick alarm buttons: +10 min, +20 min, +30 min, +1 h
- Your alarms as cards, sorted by time, showing the time, name,
  ringing days, missions and an on/off switch

Each alarm can be customized:
- Time (hour and minute wheels, 12- or 24-hour like your phone)
- Repeat: Once, Every day, Weekdays, Weekends, or any days
- Label (name)
- Snooze length: 5, 10 or 15 minutes
- Rising volume: Off, 30 s or 60 s (starts soft and grows to
  full volume)
- Wake-up missions (see below)

Wake-up missions (for people who find it hard to wake up):
The alarm only turns off after the missions are finished. Choose
one or more, each with its own settings:
- Math: Easy / Medium / Hard, 1-5 problems
- Shake: shake the phone 15 / 30 / 50 / 80 times
- Memory: repeat a pattern of 4-7 tiles
- Type: type a sentence exactly (you can write your own)
- Catch the dot: tap a moving dot 10 / 20 / 30 times
- Number order: tap 1 to 9, 16 or 25 in order
- Walk: take 20 / 50 / 100 steps (phones with a step counter)
Extra options:
- Quiet while solving: the alarm stays silent while you work on
  the mission, and rings again if you stop for 20 seconds
- Try it: test your missions right away
- With a mission there is no Stop button and Back does nothing;
  Snooze still works

Ringing:
- Full-screen alarm over the lock screen, with Snooze and Stop
  (or Start mission)
- A one-time alarm switches itself off after ringing
- Alarms stay set after a restart or an app update


==================================================================
3. STOPWATCH
==================================================================
- Watch-face dial: 60 ticks light up as the seconds pass, with an
  inner ring for the minutes of the hour
- Big time with smaller hundredths
- Status chip: Ready / Running / Paused
- Laps with live current-lap time
- Laps list: lap number, length bar, fastest (green) and slowest
  (red), best and average lap
- Count the time toward a project
- Finish saves runs of 1 minute or more to History
- Hold Reset/Finish to clear a run without saving it


==================================================================
4. PROJECTS
==================================================================
- Make a project for each subject (for example Math), with its
  own color
- Study hours are counted per project
- Project page: study stats, a 7-day chart, its timers and recent
  sessions


==================================================================
5. HISTORY AND INSIGHTS
==================================================================
- Today's study time and your streak (days in a row)
- Chart of your study time
- Month calendar: each day colored by how much you studied, with
  arrows to see other months and the month's total
- Breakdown by project
- All your sessions, with notes; add or edit sessions by hand
- Notes and #tags on sessions, with tag chips
- Focus score for each session (lower when you pause a lot or
  stop early)
- Insights such as your best time of day and longest streak
- 14 achievements (badges) in your theme color
- Weekly report every Sunday evening
- Empty charts stay hidden until your first session


==================================================================
6. BACKGROUND SOUNDS AND MUSIC
==================================================================
Plays while you focus. 12 choices:
- None
- Rain, Thunderstorm, Wind, Fireplace
- Ocean waves, Stream, Forest birds
- Fan, Brown noise
- Calm piano (nonstop playlist of 12 pieces, about 1 hour)
- Calm guitar (nonstop playlist of 8 pieces)

- Nature sounds loop seamlessly
- Sounds fade in and out gently and continue where they stopped
- Music after focus: keep the sound playing for a while after
  focus ends, with a Stop button in the notification
- All recordings are free to use; performers are credited in
  Settings


==================================================================
7. FOCUS TOOLS
==================================================================
- Silence phone while focusing: turns on Do Not Disturb during
  focus and restores it afterwards
- Strict mode: a running focus session can't be paused, reset or
  deleted (in the app, widget, tile or notification). Breaks are
  still free. In an emergency, hold "Give up" for 3 seconds to
  end the session; it won't count toward your hours.
- Distraction shield: shows a reminder screen when you open a
  distracting app during focus


==================================================================
8. NOTIFICATIONS, WIDGET AND QUICK TILE
==================================================================
- Live notification with a countdown, progress and buttons
  (Pause, +1 min, Reset; Lap for the stopwatch)
- Bigger lock-screen notification
- Home-screen widget: today's time, streak and a Start / Pause /
  Finish button. Each widget can be customized: which timer or
  project, look, stats, quick-focus length, and stopwatch mode
  when a project has no timer
- Quick Settings tile to start or pause from the notification
  shade


==================================================================
9. SETTINGS
==================================================================
- Appearance: theme, color, language, home-screen widget
- Focus: silence phone, strict mode, background sound, music
  after focus, distraction shield
- Alarm sound for timers and alarms
- Notification and alarm permissions
- Weekly report
- Export history as a CSV file
- Backup and restore all your data to a file
- Music credits and app version
